Dr. Rivera: Dorothy, your A1c came back at 7.9. We need to talk about tightening your glucose control.

Dorothy: I've been having hot flashes at night and I'm not sleeping well. Could that be messing with my sugar?

💡 Cortisol is a stress hormone that makes blood sugar harder to control. Poor sleep is a real diabetes trigger — Dorothy is right to bring this up.
Dr. Rivera: I'm going to loop in Dr. Patel about optimizing your HRT. I'd also like labs in 3 months to check your A1c and kidney function.
💡 HRT = hormone replacement therapy. Dr. Rivera is coordinating with your OB-GYN — that's exactly the right approach. Note this follow-up date.

Live session · Cardiology · Dr. Okonkwo

Visit transcript — plain language mode on

Dr. Okonkwo: Dorothy, I reviewed the BP log HealthBridge sent over. Thirty days of consistent readings around 144/88 — this is sustained Stage 2 hypertension. We need to act on this today.
💡 Stage 2 hypertension = systolic consistently above 140. It increases your risk of stroke, heart attack, and kidney damage. Treating it now matters.
Dorothy: I've been taking my Lisinopril every day. Why isn't it working?
Dr. Okonkwo: Good question. ACE inhibitors like Lisinopril can be less effective as monotherapy in African American patients — this is well documented. I want to add chlorthalidone, a thiazide-like diuretic, which has very strong evidence in your population specifically.
💡 A diuretic helps your kidneys remove extra fluid and sodium, which lowers blood pressure. Chlorthalidone is a preferred choice — strong evidence in Black patients with hypertension.
Dr. Okonkwo: I also want to order a lipid panel — your LDL hasn't been checked in 18 months. With your risk profile, we should consider a statin.
⚑ HealthBridge note: Dorothy has not been on a statin before. This may be a new medication decision — ask about benefits and side effects before agreeing.
Dorothy: What is a statin exactly? And do I really need another medication?
Dr. Okonkwo: Statins lower LDL cholesterol — the "bad" kind that clogs arteries. Let's get your lipid panel first and then decide together. Nothing is being prescribed today — I want the data first.
💡 Getting labs first before deciding is the right approach. Dorothy asked exactly the right question.
